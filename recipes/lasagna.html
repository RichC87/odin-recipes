Lasagna

The World's Best Lasagna

Here you will find the recipe to make the world's best lasagna

Ingredients

  • Beef Mince
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tinned Tomatoes
  • Lasanga Pasta Sheets
  • Mozzarella Cheese

Steps

  1. Gather your ingredients
  2. Chop onion and garlic
  3. Brown beef mince
  4. Add onion and garlic
  5. Add tinned tomatoes
  6. Layer pasta sheets and beef mixture
  7. Add mozzarella cheese on top
  8. Bake in the oven
